Rules:
- Only chart results where a chart helps: trends over time (line), comparisons across categories (bar), shares of a whole with few parts (pie). Skip the others. If nothing is worth charting, return an empty list.
- Do not write any data values. The full result of the chosen step is injected as `dataset.source`, with the column names as the first row. - Titles go in "title", in the language of the question; do not put a title inside the option.
